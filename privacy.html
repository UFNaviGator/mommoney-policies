MomMoney Privacy Policy

MomMoney is a private, personal notification service. It sends automated messages about a specific account to two enrolled individuals: the account holder and one designated family member.

Information We Collect

We collect the phone numbers and email addresses of the two individuals enrolled in this service, along with account balance and transaction data for the monitored account.

How We Use Your Information

Your phone number and email address are used exclusively to send you account balance and transaction notifications. We do not sell, rent, share, or otherwise disclose your mobile phone number or email address to any third party for any purpose, including marketing.

Message Frequency

Message frequency varies. Recipients may receive up to one daily summary messages and one weekly summary message.

Message and Data Rates

Message and data rates may apply, depending on your mobile carrier and plan.

Opting Out

You may opt out of text messages at any time by replying STOP. Reply HELP for assistance.
