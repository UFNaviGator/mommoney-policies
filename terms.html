MomMoney Terms of Service

MomMoney is a private, personal account-monitoring notification service operated by an individual for the purpose of sending automated balance and spending updates for a single account to two enrolled recipients.

Description of Service

Enrolled recipients receive text and/or email messages containing account balance information and transaction summaries.

Consent and Enrollment

Both recipients have given direct, verbal consent to receive these messages and are personally known to the service operator.

Message Frequency

Message frequency varies. Recipients may receive up to one message per day and one weekly summary message.

Message and Data Rates

Message and data rates may apply, depending on your mobile carrier and plan.

Opt-Out

Reply STOP to any message to opt out at any time. Reply HELP for assistance.

No Warranty
